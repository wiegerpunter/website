About

I am a PhD researcher at Eindhoven University of Technology, working on algorithms and data management for high-volume data streams. My research focuses on designing scalable, memory-efficient methods for analysing multidimensional and continuously evolving data. My work combines theoretical algorithm design with practical implementation in data-intensive systems. I am also interested in applying these methods to interdisciplinary challenges, including the analysis of environmental and geospatial data.
